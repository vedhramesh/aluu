# aluu

Flashcards for about 280 core words, phrases and word-endings of West Greenlandic (Kalaallisut), for complete beginners.

- **Hand-picked core, not a frequency list.** Greenlandic builds long words from a root plus endings ("I can speak" is one word, oqalussinnaavunga), so the deck pairs everyday words and phrases with the most useful endings: have, not, want, can, will, in, to, from, my.
- **Pronunciation signals.** q, r and ll (the sounds English doesn't have) are colored. Bars over each word show syllables, tall for long (double) vowels.
- **Spaced repetition, Anki-style**, with mixed listening and speaking cards, a Don't know loop, and a quiz (listening questions can be turned off).
- **Hover or tap any word in an example** to see what it means.
- **Works offline** and can be installed to your phone's home screen.

## Audio

No phone or browser comes with a Greenlandic voice. The app uses Martha, the free Greenlandic voice from Greenland's language secretariat (atuffat.gl), if it's installed on the device. Without it the app still works: listening cards show the word, and the quiz skips listening questions.

## Caveat

This word list hasn't been checked by a native speaker. Treat the examples as a starting point and check them with a Greenlandic speaker if you can.

Progress is stored in your browser only. Settings has export and import.
